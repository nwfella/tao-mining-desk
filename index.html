    How every number is computed (and what it does not include)

    Sources

    Chain state read through the public, keyless metagraph.sh API: per-subnet /economics, /chain/burn, /chain/emission-pipeline, /chain/deregistration-ranking, the registry list, and a per-UID /yield table for all 129 subnets. TAO/USD is that API's wrapped-onchain median.

    Nothing here is a projection or a score. Every figure below is either measured or arithmetic on measured values, and anything that could not be measured is shown as — rather than zero.

    Income per winning miner

    Σ miner emission_α × epochs/day × α price × TAO/USD ÷ winning miners

    The per-UID emission table is what the chain actually pays each hotkey in one tempo. Epochs per day come from the subnet's own tempo (86400 ÷ tempo × 12s), so a 360-block subnet gets 20 epochs/day and a 99-block subnet about 72. This is an average across miners that earn anything — it is not what the median miner earns, and it is certainly not what a new UID earns. The median column below is the median among miners that earn something, not among all registered UIDs; the zero-emission majority is excluded from both, which is why the winning-miner count sits next to them.

    Payback

    burn × TAO/USD ÷ income per winning miner

    Days of gross emission to recover the registration burn alone. It excludes hardware, hosting, electricity, dev time, deregistration losses and alpha price movement on the way out. A subnet that shows 3 days payback is a subnet where the average winning miner at today's price and today's emission rate would clear the burn in 3 days — treat it as an upper bound on attractiveness, never as a return.

    Why "winning miners" is the first column to read

    Most registered UIDs earn exactly zero. The zero-emission share is measured, not inferred, and it is routinely above 90% on competitive subnets. A subnet with 250 miners and 8 earning UIDs is paying 8 seats; the other 242 are paying burn to sit in a chair that does not pay. The bar in that column is the earning share.

    Prune risk

    Subnets are deregistered by moving-price rank when a new one registers. Rank 1 is next to go. Immunity covers the first stretch after registration and is not a guarantee of income. If a subnet you mine is rank 1–5, your UID and your alpha go with it.

    What this desk cannot see

    • Hardware you actually need to win — the registry's declared floor is a floor to run, not to earn.
    • Your share if you join: emission is metered by validator weights, and a new UID starts at zero.
    • Slippage on the way in or out of alpha, and the validator permit floor for staking.
    • Anything about a subnet's real revenue — emission is subsidy until proven otherwise.
